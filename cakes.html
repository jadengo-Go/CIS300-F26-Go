Coffee Cake Roll

Ingredients:

  • 6 tsp instant coffee
  • 100g cake flour
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 6 egg yolk (whites seperated)
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract


  • 6 egg whites
  • 1/2 cup sugar


  • 3/4 cup heavy cream (sub: Silk Dairy-Free Heavy Cream)
  • 2 tsp instant coffee
  • 6 tsp sugar
  • 2 tsp water

Steps:

  1. Mix everything in the first section in a large metal bowl
  2. Beat the egg whites into a fully mixture with stiff peaks. Slowly add in the 1/2 cup of sugar while beating the eggs.
  3. Fold in the egg whites into the metal bowl
  4. Bake folded mixture at 350 degrees
  5. While baking, take the third set of ingredients and dissolve the sugar and coffee into the water. Then, beat the heavy cream into stiff peaks. Add the sugar and coffee mixture slowly into the heavy cream.
  6. Allow the cake to cool
  7. Once cool, place the cream on top of the cake and begin to roll
  8. Cool in the fridge for 2 hours
  9. Cut and serve the newly made cake roll
